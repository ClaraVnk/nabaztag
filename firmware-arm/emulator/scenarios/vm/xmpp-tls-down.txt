# bytecode: bootloader
#
# The warren goes down under a TLS session and comes back much later.
#
# The stock gate tries the XMPP port three times, then falls back to BOSH
# (XMPP over HTTP, port 80), which no warren serves, and only leaves BOSH
# when a BOSH attempt fails -- an HTTP answer is not a failure. On
# 2026-11-10 a rabbit sat there two hours and ten minutes.
# bytecode/patch_liveness.py removes the fallback: three tries, a wait of
# 5 s plus up to 30 s, three more, for ever.
#
# Run as boot 2 of tools/xmpp-tls-proof.sh with --down-at 100 --up-at 330:
# the Terrier container stops 100 s in (its sockets close with it) and
# starts again at 330 s. The verdict is a TLS session registered after the
# restart, and no port 80 open in between.
ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 open -52
tick 50
stop 480000

at 20000  expect RUN
at 470000 expect RUN
