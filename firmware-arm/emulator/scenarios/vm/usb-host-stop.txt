# The USB host controller stops under a running rabbit (usb_watch, 2026-11-20).
#
# On Nabazprout the JTAG wires knocked the USB chip into reset while the ARM
# ran on: the frame number froze, the radio went silent, and the rabbit
# scanned on channel 1 until the mains. The controller runs here for 12 s --
# no reset may come from the watch while it does -- then stops; the firmware
# must restart the rabbit with reason 5 about 5 s later.
# tools/usb-watch-proof.sh checks the log.
config-mode
ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 open -52
tick 50
stop 25000

at 12000 expect-no-reset
at 12000 usb-host-stop
