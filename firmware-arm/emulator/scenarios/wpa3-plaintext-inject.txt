# The same forged plaintext frames (see wpa2-plaintext-inject.txt), on a WPA3
# link. SAE protects the password; what it does not protect, without this,
# is the downlink: with plaintext data accepted the uplink is encrypted and
# the downlink can be written by anyone. A group rekey and a pairwise rekey,
# both encrypted, still go through.

ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 wpa3-sae -52
psk nabaztag
conf obj/conf/wpa3.eeprom
tick 50
stop 90000

at 20000 expect RUN
at 25000 inject-plaintext
at 26000 expect-plain-dropped 1
at 30000 inject-plaintext eapol
at 31000 expect-plain-dropped 2
at 40000 rekey
at 50000 ptk-rekey
at 89000 expect RUN
