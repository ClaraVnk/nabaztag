# WPA2 with CCMP, which is what every modern access point does.
#
# The whole chain: the beacon's cipher suites are read, the comparison in
# OPnetAuth (vinterp.c:921) lets netAuth run, the four-way handshake
# completes, and the group key the AP wrapped with the KEK is unwrapped and
# installed. That last step is what crypto/aes128.c and the wpa2_gtk
# modification exist for, and before this emulator it could only be checked
# by flashing a rabbit and watching whether broadcast traffic ever arrived.

ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 wpa2-ccmp -52
psk nabaztag
tick 50
stop 60000

at 10000 expect RUN
