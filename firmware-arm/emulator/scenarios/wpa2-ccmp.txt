# WPA2 with CCMP, which is what every modern AP does. Two things are under
# test here and only the first one passes today:
#
#   1. the beacon's cipher suites are read correctly, and the comparison in
#      OPnetAuth (vinterp.c:921) lets netAuth run at all
#   2. the four-way handshake completes
#
# The virtual AP cannot run a four-way handshake yet, so the rabbit reaches
# IEEE80211_S_EAPOL and stops there. That is what this asserts: the point is
# to lock in step 1 and make step 2's absence visible.

ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 wpa2-ccmp -52
psk nabaztag
tick 50
stop 60000

at 10000 expect EAPOL
