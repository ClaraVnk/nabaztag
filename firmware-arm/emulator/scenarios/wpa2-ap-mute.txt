# The access point forgets a WPA2 rabbit and swallows its frames.
#
# wpa2-ap-forget.txt's AP still says reason 7 when the forgotten station
# transmits. Some do not: a station on the wrong side of an ACL, or an AP
# whose driver drops class 3 frames from unknown stations without a word,
# gives nothing back at all -- and the beacons keep coming. This is the
# case nothing but a watchdog can find, and the one IEEE80211_LINK_DEAD_MS
# (180 s without one unicast data frame from the AP) exists for: RUN since
# 5 s, forgotten at 20 s, probes from 65 s unanswered, the association
# given up at 185 s, RUN again a few seconds later.
ap 02:00:5e:10:00:01 Terrier 6 wpa2-ccmp -52
psk nabaztag
tick 50
stop 240000
at 10000  expect RUN
at 20000  forget mute
at 180000 expect RUN
at 180000 expect-reassoc 1
at 230000 expect RUN
at 230000 expect-reassoc 2
